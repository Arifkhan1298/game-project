Authoritative Game Rules & Guides

Complete reference documentation for all six strategic disciplines.

♟ Standard Chess

Objective: Place the opponent's King under attack such that no legal escape move exists (Checkmate).
Special Rules Implemented:
• Castling: King moves two squares toward a rook that has not yet moved, provided no squares between are occupied or attacked.
• En Passant: A pawn advancing two squares may be captured diagonally as if it had only moved one square by an enemy pawn on the adjacent file immediately on the following move.
• Promotion: Reaching the 8th rank allows choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
• Draws: Stalemate, 50-move rule, threefold repetition, and insufficient mating material.

🔴 Connect Four

Objective: Be the first player to form a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of four consecutive discs in a 7-column by 6-row vertical grid.
Physics: Discs drop by gravity to the lowest unoccupied space in the chosen column.

⚪ Reversi (Othello)

Objective: Have the majority of your colored discs on the 8x8 board at game end.
Rules: Each move must trap one or more opponent discs between your newly placed disc and another existing disc of your color. Trapped discs are flipped. If you have no legal move, your turn is passed.

💣 Minesweeper

Objective: Reveal all safe cells on the grid without detonating any hidden mines.
Guaranteed Safe Click: The first click is 100% safe, clearing an automatic 3x3 zone.
Controls: Left-click to reveal. Right-click (or flag button) to plant/remove hazard flags. Numbers denote exact adjacent mine counts.

🃏 Memory Match

Objective: Match all identical pairs of strategic relics in the fewest moves and fastest time. Unmatched card pairs flip face-down after a brief inspection window.

✕ Tic-Tac-Toe

Objective: Place three matching marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line on the 3x3 grid. Hard mode features an optimal unyielding Minimax agent.

🔢 2048 Number Merge

Objective: Slide tiles across the 4x4 grid. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one with double the value. Reach the legendary 2048 tile to achieve victory!

🐍 Cyber Retro Snake

Objective: Pilot the cyber serpent across the digital grid. Consume neon nutrients (+10 pts) and rare Golden Power Gems (+50 pts) to grow longer while avoiding collisions with perimeter barriers and your own tail.

👑 Checkers (Draughts)

Objective: Capture all opponent checkers or leave them with no legal moves. Jump over enemy pieces diagonally to capture them. Reach the furthest row to crown your piece as a King with multi-directional mobility!

🧩 Sudoku Master

Objective: Fill the 9x9 grid so every row, column, and 3x3 box contains all digits from 1 to 9 without duplication. Features pencil notes, real-time conflict checking, and tactical hints.

🧱 Neon Tetris (Brick Fall)

Objective: Rotate and place falling tetromino blocks to form complete horizontal lines without gaps. Features ghost projection, piece holding (Hold queue), and high-level drop speed acceleration.

🏓 Cyber Pong

Objective: Deflect the high-speed cyber orb past the opponent's paddle. Utilize paddle spin and angular velocity to outmaneuver the reactive AI. First to 5 points claims match victory!

🔤 Wordle Guess

Objective: Deduce the secret 5-letter word in 6 attempts or fewer. Green tiles indicate correct letters in the correct position; Yellow indicates correct letters in the wrong position; Gray indicates letters not present.

💡 Simon Says Memory

Objective: Memorize and replicate increasingly complex sequences of synthesized audio-visual pulses across four glowing neon quadrants.

🚢 Battleship Fleet Radar

Objective: Command a naval fleet on a 10x10 radar grid. Strategically position your 5 warships and launch artillery scans to locate and sink the enemy fleet before your own is eliminated.

🕹️ Arcade Breakout

Objective: Demolish layers of colorful bricks with bouncing kinetic energy orbs. Catch falling power-up capsules (Widened Paddle, Multi-Ball, Laser Cannons) and clear the sector before losing your 3 lives.
